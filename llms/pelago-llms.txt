# Pelago

> Pelago is a virtual-first digital clinic for substance use disorder, delivering personalized treatment for tobacco, alcohol, opioid, cannabis, and stimulant use. It combines digital cognitive behavioral tools, live clinical counseling, and medication-assisted treatment (PelagoRX) with same-day nationwide access. Pelago sells primarily to employers and health plans as a covered behavioral-health benefit. Formerly Quit Genius; backed by Atomico and EQT Ventures. Pelago publishes a marketing and member-enrollment web presence but exposes no public developer API surface.

## Compliance & Security
- HITRUST, SOC-2, and LegitScript certified
- HIPAA and 42 CFR Part 2 (confidentiality of substance-use-disorder records) aligned
- GDPR and CCPA compliant
- The Joint Commission Gold Seal of Approval
- Encryption at rest and in transit; third-party penetration testing; segregated client data storage
